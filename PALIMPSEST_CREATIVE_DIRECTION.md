## Identity and purpose — authoritative

PALIMPSEST is a **genuinely beautiful, interactive, three-dimensional digital pop-up history book about the All in Starsilk**. It is presented as a prestigious **Administration instructional edition**, not an impartial omniscient history, a game dashboard, a generic 3D gallery, or an unrelated puzzle anthology.

Its reader should feel that they are physically opening, handling, turning, lifting, folding, exploring, and operating an exquisite manufactured book. Visually and mechanically it should recover the wonder, page presence, dimensional depth, and quality of the earliest successful prototype, rather than merely reproduce its feature checklist. The exact earlier visual reference must be positively identified and compared before claiming fidelity.

## The Administration's truthful but selective history — authoritative

- The Administration's statements must remain consistent with **established Starsilk events and mechanics**. The reader is not to be taught invented or counterfactual events as facts.
- The presentation is **meaningfully deceptive through omission, framing, selection, scale, labels, sequence, and emphasis**. Its choice of what to commemorate and what to leave undiscussed is consequential.
- Administration achievements, technical competence, and official procedures may be depicted beautifully and sincerely. The cost may be reduced to authorized totals, kept off the public plate, framed institutionally, or left unmentioned where doing so remains factually honest; never rewrite an established event to fabricate a triumph.
- The public narrator is **warm, assured, conversational, accessible, and mid-century instructional**, sincerely speaking from within the Administration's understanding. The narrator must not wink at the audience, confess the propaganda, morally editorialize from an outside perspective, perform knowingly sinister pauses, or react to concealed testimony.
- A visitor who never finds secret material must still encounter a complete, coherent, wondrous Administration history. Attentive readers may infer omissions from source-grounded contrasts and the world shown, without being lectured that the book is deceptive.
- Public presentation, maker testimony, historical canon, and design-only editorial metaphors remain separate knowledge layers. Never promote an editorial metaphor into a literal cosmological fact.
- The Administration's exact closing slogan stays **"Your fate is in our hands."**
- Assess the existing archival manuscript and any proposed narration against this framing rule, but **do not modify historical text, approved scripts, or canon without a separate source review and user approval**. A suspected mismatch is a review item, not permission to rewrite.

## Physical and visual standard — authoritative

- **One continuous twelve-folio volume (F01–F12)**. Do not revert to a three-folio preview or require a handoff to a second renderer mid-book. Preserve the separate archival edition as an explicit optional counterpart.
- **Book first:** initial arrival presents a physically **closed** cover. The reader deliberately opens it; page turns are continuous manipulations of a real-looking deforming cardstock leaf, not a scene-swap disguised as a swipe.
- Every main historical scene and its source-authored A/B/C mechanism should be materially tethered to the page by folds, hinges, tracks, pull-tabs, rivets, layered paper, or another credible visual connection.
- Digital motion may become grander, deeper, and spatially stranger than real paper permits, **provided its material origin remains legible**. Exceed physics; do not abandon the pop-up-book identity.
- Build with dimensional layering, cut-paper silhouettes, visible page thickness, coherent shadows, rich cel/ink palettes, precise typography, materially plausible fold motion, and source-specific composition. Avoid generic floating 3D objects, gratuitous glow/particles, glossy plastic CGI, monotonous backdrops, or spectacle unrelated to historical meaning.
- Art direction must be judged by actual rendered desktop and touch-sized views, against identified original-prototype visual evidence and a common quality rubric. Source tests alone cannot prove beauty, wonder, or tactile delight.

## Interaction and UI — authoritative

- **The book itself is the primary interface:** cover, page corners, tabs, hinges, pull-outs, pop-ups, physical figures, paper diagrams, and optional inspection.
- No ordinary web-app chrome should compete with the book at rest. The **only persistent conventional on-screen control is a discreet bottom-center chevron** opening and closing the minimum necessary controls. The existing physical-book interaction remains available when the drawer is closed.
- Collapsing the chevron must restore an unobstructed book. The control can use an invisible generous touch target without becoming visually dominant.
- Conventional camera, reading, chapter, mechanism alternatives and accessibility tools may live inside the expanded control surface; they must remain operable by keyboard/screen reader and on mobile, and remain available in a robust non-WebGL equivalent. Minimal UI **never means deleting essential access**.
- Preserve reduced-motion semantics, meaningful focus and status announcements, safe pointer cancellation, responsive layout, and a no-flash alternative where needed.
- No new persistent side-mode, sighting puzzle, mini-game, HUD, or optical-comparison apparatus is required. Such ideas remain **unapproved candidates** and must prove they strengthen the core book experience before adoption. The product name is **PALIMPSEST**.

## Non-negotiable source and behavior locks

1. Preserve the original historical manuscript, archival edition, saved archival data, and the existing independent Living Book study storage. Do not migrate or combine without explicit approval.
2. Preserve all twelve authored folios and **all thirty-six A/B/C contracts**, including source-specific models, DRAXIS bounds, chronology, and accessible reading equivalents.
3. F01 remains strictly pre-stellar and protects the exact maker testimony **"He murdered her."**, revealed only by authorized intimate inspection beneath Wordstreamer; never the public narrator.
4. F02 introduces the first stars and protects the exact Geode-inside testimony **"NiAlBu still watches her die. Again and again."**; no public acknowledgment or secret-driven audio change.
5. F03 retains bounded inherited planet-template variation; proposed third inscription, changed archival narration, and unresolved chronology remain unapproved.
6. F05/B retains four stages of Blood Ring manufacture, with physical vitrified matter rather than a mystical glow or halo.
7. F09/A remains the **irreversible** collapse of an explicitly nonhistorical MODEL STAR for the session; never confuse it with later Starbinding.
8. F10's inhabited-world Siege Wall presentation remains source-bounded; F11 may not invent life outside its authorized mechanism.
9. Existing authored character figures are editorial book assets, **not new approvals of canonical artwork**.
10. Retain one renderer/animation-loop ownership, safe scene disposal, closed-cover arrival, direct page-turn gestures, and full fallbacks.

## Creative acceptance — the test that matters

The design succeeds only when the reader can say, without seeing a UI explanation: **"I am handling a magnificent pop-up book. Its lessons feel authoritative. Its paper is alive. This is the Administration's version of what happened."**

A source-complete page that feels like a conventional website, flat slideshow, generic 3D exhibit, or an unearned visual-effects montage **does not pass**. Nor does a cinematic spectacle that ignores the book's physical construction.

## Authority, scope, and supersession

This user-approved experiential charter governs **future PALIMPSEST design decisions** once accepted into the active repository baseline. It supersedes incompatible *creative goals* in older proposals, including the abandoned obligation to build an experience titled "The Thickness of History." It does **not** delete earlier documents or supersede their factual canon locks, explicitly accepted source assets, record provenance, historical manuscript, or prior verified behavior.

The current source may not yet comply in full. Capture disagreements as a bounded audit and user review; do not silently reframe the historical manuscript or erase earlier development evidence. Read the [recovery plan](PALIMPSEST_RECOVERY_PLAN.md) before implementing.
