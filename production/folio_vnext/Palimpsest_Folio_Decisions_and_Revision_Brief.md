## F11 · Required sequence

1. **Set the timeline:** The war ended just **31 days ago**. Show that postwar work is already underway and that people with blood on their hands are trying to clean up. This is framing, not an Administration confession or disclosure of what happened.
2. **Codec appears first.** Keep the barren strata and loam vessel/lift as the visual starting point.
3. **Marcel arrives second.** Codec and Marcel are happy. Marcel’s gravity and Wordstreaming effects are present and visible on arrival.
4. **The dirt works before Tiger enters.** After Marcel’s arrival, the dirt spreads and the page erupts into a vibrant jungle. Plants must visibly grow from paper: roots/creases establish contact with the page, then stems, leaves and canopy rise in a fast, legible burst. Give the fully changed spread a beat on its own.
5. **Tiger, the Shard-God, arrives last.** Codec becomes sad; Marcel and Tiger remain celebratory. The official account does not disclose the memory or claim the Administration knows it. Let the contrast in reactions carry that information.

## Effects progression · establish paper, then break its rules deliberately

- **F01:** dark table, scored paper, hard silhouettes and restrained physical movement. No glow bloom, particles or liquid-ink spectacle. If a design has a luminous feature, use dark-and-colored paper layering rather than projected light.
- **F02:** the first impossible effect. White vellum → spreading black ink → stars and galaxies over ink. Keep the paper edges and folds readable.
- **F03–F06:** move from physical templates and globes to synchronized planet lights, independent orbital motion and shimmer, then a brief mechanical response to the timeline dial. Quiet beats provide contrast; effects do not run at full strength continuously.
- **F07–F11:** raise the ceiling deliberately: layered vellum shadows, event-triggered portal plasma, a strong center-seeking gravitational pull, the wall drawing figures into the black, then F11’s full-spread jungle eruption. A strong effect always has a physical anchor and a readable ending.
- **F12 and the coda:** the role cycle can be fully theatrical, but it cannot make the records illegible. End on one restrained, physical pop-out for the slogan rather than trying to outdo F11. Reduced-motion variants preserve cause, narrative order and final state.

## Blender character pass · required before candidate sign-off

All 12 candidate scenes reopened with mechanism actions. A scan of action names found no identifiable reusable character blink, wink or idle clips; it did not establish that the faces are already rigged. Do the following in **Blender on new candidate copies**, before export, not as a to-do for the web port.

1. **Inventory the on-page actors.** Record figure/version, eye layout, source facial features, and acting beats per spread. Apply this to all live eyed or emoting actors, including new additions. Printed portraits, diagram symbols and the character-free coda are not live actors.
2. **Rig blink, wink and expression from canonical geometry.** For faces with two independently movable eyes, add left/right closure controls and separate Blink, Wink_L and Wink_R clips. Blink occasionally in idle; wink only on a deliberate cue. Preserve gaze and silhouette. For Tiger, close the pupil-less cyan slits with their dark crystal edges; do not add pupils, human lids or teeth. For nonhuman, masked or eyeless designs, use a source-appropriate slit/plate, brow, head angle or posture instead of inventing anatomy. F01 facial motion still has to read literally as moving paper.
3. **Author editable character idles and story actions.** Make each live figure a named Blender Action with gentle body-specific weight shifts, paper flex, gaze or holds. Offset idle timings so a group does not blink in unison. Keep face and body idles separate from the scene event tracks; fade or pause idles under story cues and resume cleanly. Preserve the candidate originals and make a per-figure action manifest.
4. **Prove the important cues.** F01: restrained, physical standee acting. F05: Drakken idles independent of ring orbit and planet rotation. F07: idles for live relief figures. F08: Marcel’s acting reads through portal activation. F10: Drakken idle before the cue, then react and get drawn in. F11: Codec and Marcel happy before the jungle; Tiger enters only after growth, Codec turns sad, and Marcel/Tiger stay celebratory. On F12, only physically staged live role figures need an acting rig.
5. **Acceptance proof, not just names in a file.** Reopen revised Blender copies and preview each live figure both at normal reading distance and in close-up. Verify an independent left/right wink where applicable, clean bilateral closure, any required emotional transitions, offset idle timing, consistent paper geometry and seamless re-entry after scene actions. F11 gets an uncut timing preview. Retain the rig/action manifest and proof renders.

## Motion, testing and implementation order

- **Motion grammar:** Give each folio a subtle idle appropriate to its paper and subject. Reserve stronger motion for actions that cause a meaningful change: F02 ink, F08 portal activation, F09 inward pull, F10 wall capture, F11 jungle growth, and the F12 role cycle. Reduced motion should show equivalent, readable end states without rapid shimmer or motion.
- **First prototype: F02.** This is the best test of the project’s visual rule. Use one Living Book spread with a visible edge of ink advancing across white vellum, and verify that the first stars appear only over ink. Check start, mid-spread and fully expanded, plus backward navigation/re-entry and a tablet-width touch pass.
- **Second prototype: F11, as a rough sequence before full effects.** Prove the timing of Codec → Marcel and gravity/Wordstreaming → dirt/jungle → Tiger and the emotional split. If the story order is not clear with low-detail blocking, additional foliage won’t fix it.
- **Run the Blender character pass alongside the F02/F11 proofs.** Then prototype the margin objects at book scale and apply the bounded asset and mechanism changes, followed by an isolated F12-to-coda page-turn test. Verify Living Book and Archive separately: several later areas share existing folio classes, so these changes must be opt-in from the Living Book. Preserve navigation, original assets, and fallback states.
- **Still to source, not to invent:** the exact F07 support imagery, the F12 role roster/wording, and named source templates for the initial F03 globes. The role choices and support imagery may be proposed and verified without delaying a reversible F02 or F11 proof.
