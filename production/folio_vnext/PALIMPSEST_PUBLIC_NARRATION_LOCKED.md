# PALIMPSEST: AN ILLUSTRATED HISTORY OF THE ALL

## Administration Instructional Edition — Proposed Narration

### PROLOGUE

**NARRATOR:** Welcome to *Palimpsest: An Illustrated History of the All*.

The models are small. The events they explain were not.

We shall take them one page at a time.

### F01 — BEFORE THE STARS

**NARRATOR:** Before the first stars, there were three divine principles. We call them the Triumvirate.

Wordstreamer gave language its living possibility. NiAlBu held memory and recurrence.

And Tiger, the Shard-God, concerned himself with order and consequence.

We have placed them side by side so you can see them clearly. You should not take that to mean they stood together.

The original arrangement did not last. The All, however, continued.

In time, the Administration would take responsibility for much of the work of keeping it in order.

### F02 — THE ALL UNFOLDS

**NARRATOR:** At first, there is only the page. A very plain page indeed.

Watch the dark ink spread across the vellum.

There! The first point of light.

And another. Then a cluster. Soon, more stars than you could possibly count!

If the page seems too small, try opening the folds.

There we are.

What looked empty a moment ago has a shape. And what a shape it is!

### F03 — THE NOTEBOOK PROGRAM

**NARRATOR:** Now, here is a particularly clever arrangement.

The WorldsVault contains inherited planetary templates. The Notebook Program works from them to produce variations.

Choose a source plate. Now turn the die.

There is your globe!

A second impression may share the same source without being quite the same.

Choose another template, and the result changes again.

The possibilities are considerable. But nothing here begins from nothing.

### F04 — THE GREAT MISUNDERSTANDING

*New historical folio*

**NARRATOR:** The Notebook did not stop with planets.

In the Aureal Nebula, one remarkable result was Mother: a living world.

From Mother came the Drakken, carried in dark Eggs and equipped to reshape planetary environments.

On some inhabited worlds, those Eggs were mistaken for divine visitors.

People built temples around them. Quite carefully, too.

Now lift the little roof.

Oh! Look at the ground beneath it.

The landscape is already changing.

The historical record calls this the Great Misunderstanding.

It did not remain a misunderstanding for long.

### F05 — YEAR ZERO: FIRST CONTACT

**NARRATOR:** Year Zero. Here the Blood Eclipse War begins.

Drakken terraforming reached the fringe colonies. The Administration answered with counter-Macros.

A great many people took part in that response.

Watch the lights. Each new one marks a world reached by the conflict.

The lights make a tidy pattern on our page.

The fighting was rather less tidy.

### F06 — YEAR THREE: THE STANDARD OF CONQUEST

**NARRATOR:** In Year Three, we come to Fallenstar Prime.

It was an inhabited world long before the ring was constructed above it.

The Drakken removed material from its biosphere and fashioned an enormous orbital structure.

Watch the manufacturing stages unfold as the C.R.o.W.N. installation takes shape.

The finished Blood Ring is not colored light. It is matter, transformed and hardened.

Turn the model. Notice how the planet and its ring move separately.

And remember: the material in orbit came from the world below.

### F07 — THE LONG ATTRITION

**NARRATOR:** Now turn the dial.

My, how quickly a century passes on paper!

For more than a hundred years, the front moved, held, and moved again.

The ledger contains some figures. Others have not been recorded.

Here is an empty box.

It tells us no number has been entered. It does not tell us the number was zero.

A blank is not a zero. It is countless stories unrecorded.

That's a useful thing to remember when reading history.

### F08 — YEAR ONE HUNDRED TWENTY-ONE: THE SIEGE OF THE RUBY ECLIPSE

**NARRATOR:** In Year One Hundred Twenty-One, the Administration advanced into the Pharos Nebula.

Here is the plan.

A plan has arrows, and every arrow points somewhere.

But an arrow does not carry supplies. People must do that.

Lift the plan and examine the arrangements beneath it.

Routes. Equipment. Places to regroup.

A campaign, you see, is a great deal more than its map.

### F09 — YEAR ONE HUNDRED SEVENTY: THE AUREAL GATE

**NARRATOR:** Year One Hundred Seventy.

Here we come to the Aureal Gate, part of the Hookshot Network.

At this gate, the Drakken used Starsilk against the Shard-God.

What had seemed beyond injury could be wounded.

This changed the calculations of the war.

Now open the foldout.

Twenty inhabited worlds were newly taken under Drakken control at this turning point.

The little marks are uniform for printing.

The worlds were not.

### F10 — THE HAL’VEN HELIOCIDE

**NARRATOR:** This is a model star. It is not one of the suns lost in the Hal’Ven Cluster.

Starsilk runs through every stellar center.

Watch what happens when the filament is pulled from our model.

Notice how the nearby paper bends toward the center.

There. The model star has collapsed. It cannot be restored.

At Hal’Ven, the Shard-God carried out this operation on an enormous scale.

Star after star. Thousands of suns.

Each became a black hole.

Together, those singularities formed the basis of the Siege Wall.

### F11 — THE SIEGE WALL AND POSTWAR PROTOCOLS

**NARRATOR:** A wall is a straightforward thing to draw.

The Siege Wall, however, is made from black holes, not bricks.

From an inhabited world, it does not look like a wall at all.

It looks like a piece of the night from which the stars have gone missing.

It held the Drakken inside. It also enclosed other lives on the far side.

The advance stopped. The Blood Eclipse War ended.

Afterward, the Administration prohibited the study or repetition of the heliocide method.

This little model explains the historical event. It is not an instruction for repeating it.

### F12 — THIRTY-ONE DAYS LATER: THE FIRST DIRT

**NARRATOR:** Thirty-one days after the war, the Administration was already at work on Nacreous VI.

The planet was a remnant, its land badly damaged by the fighting.

One of our chief combat macro engineers had taken battle Macros and turned them toward a different purpose.

There is living soil in that little cylinder.

It is about the length of your forearm.

Not enough to restore a planet. But enough to grow wheat.

Together, veterans of the Blood Eclipse War join the work.

Now, let's see what our illustrated garden might become!

Oh, my! Our paper has managed an entire jungle!

A real world would take considerably longer.

The actual achievement was much smaller. And it was real.

A little living soil. A few blades of wheat.

A new beginning worth studying indeed!

### F13 — THE UNFINISHED PEACE

**ADMINISTRATION VOICE:** The Drakken advance has been contained.

Even so, the work of preservation continues.

There are worlds to survey, records to keep, and people to teach.

Observe the different duties represented by our little figures.

Each requires skill, patience, and a willingness to contribute.

Perhaps one of these duties will someday be yours.

There is a place for you in that work.

The Administration invites you to serve.

### FINAL POP-OUT — CLOSING IMPRINT

**ADMINISTRATION VOICE:** Your fate is in our hands.
