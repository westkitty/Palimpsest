# PALIMPSEST — The Living Theatre
## Visual uplift contract · 20 November 2026

**Status:** implemented source-grounded editorial papercraft on feature branch; visual equivalence on real devices remains unverified. This is a visual supplement to the existing source-authored scene geometry, not a new story or replacement for the archival reader.

### Existing foundation
The currently released Living Folios already navigate F01–F12 in one scene runtime. F04–F12 reuse the authored `BaseFolioWorld` subclasses and A/B/C mechanisms. F01–F03 have separate sculptural design and protected hidden testimony. The archival historical edition and its narration remain separate. Completing navigation did not prove equal theatrical richness, camera staging or physical-device visual fidelity.

### Three divergent challengers
1. **Applied luminescence:** increase vignette, glow, particles, exposure. Easy to implement but has no new causal or historical meaning. **Rejected** as spectacle; also risks flattening cel-paint and reducing readability.
2. **Guided cinematic camera:** scripted close-ups to existing figures and mechanisms. Could improve legibility, but may wrest control from inspection, introduce motion discomfort and obscure layout. **Deferred**, not necessary to the current test.
3. **Paper that remembers the mechanism:** give each later folio a distinct, die-cut architectural backdrop with independently hinged A/B/C layers. Its visual structure changes as the reader operates the *existing* authored mechanisms. The staging advances the story through material and agency rather than glows or additional lore. **Chosen bounded experiment.**

### Implemented visual grammar
- Three independent, physically bounded extruded paper profiles per later folio, mounted on visible mechanical brass hinges behind the source-authored model. Each has its own ink contours, muted palette, cut-paper skyline, relief depth and source-specific contour vocabulary.
- The three leaf pivots follow A/B/C *without adding new story state*. Reduced motion jumps directly to final positions.
- F04: changing colonial skyline versus the countermeasure demonstration.
- F05: ominous crimson manufacture relief; four discrete paper marks track the existing documented B pipeline stages, not additional Drakken.
- F06: asymmetrical fractured intervals reflect accumulating loss without inventing casualty totals.
- F07: splintered campaign-line relief; failure is spatial rather than an invented victory animation.
- F08: monumental hinged gate silhouette expresses divine vulnerability without depicting new Starsilk events.
- F09: an editorial MODEL STAR emblem is replaced by actual dark paper absence when the existing irreversible A operation is triggered; it never reverses within the session. This is the Hal'Ven heliocide, **not** Codec's later Starbinding.
- F10: the in-world B state shows one pitch-black horizon interruption, never a visible gridded Siege Wall from an inhabited world.
- F11: barren ochre paper crater relief never grows plants outside Marcel's single protected loam-cylinder demonstration.
- F12: a physical split in the paper backdrop keeps an unfinished public record from looking like a triumphal ending.

### Source, semantics and protections
- Implemented in `src/vnext/livingStagecraft.ts` and integrated with `src/vnext/previewV2.ts`. The original nine authored stage classes, figures and DRAXIS models remain the source of their own characters and mechanisms.
- No new characters, planets, Drakken strains, timeline events, music, narration, secrets, canon approvals or revisions to the historical manuscript.
- F01 must remain pre-stellar; F02 remains the first stars; F03 remains bounded source-template variation. The two exact early secret phrases and their inspection gates remain unaffected.
- The preview renderer retains its one animation loop, camera controls, accessible schematic fallback, local session state, capped device pixel ratio and hardware/software rendering distinction.
- New display layers are not gameplay controls and must not intercept genuine hit targets. They must dispose geometry, lines and materials on page turn and teardown.

### Acceptance gates and limits
1. One page per F01–F12, no forced transfer into archival reader.
2. Original source-specific A/B/C mechanisms remain individually operable; exact irreversible F09 action remains locked.
3. Each of F04–F12 has three own independently hinged profiles; F05, F09 and F10 extra effects agree with their source state; no new life on F11.
4. Full TypeScript, contract tests, source tests, production build, and built Chromium smoke pass on the **final** commit.
5. A physically inspected desktop and touch-device visual sweep, GPU lifecycle stability and firsthand impression of theatrical uplift remain **UNVERIFIED** until observed. Green source tests are not human art approval.

### Honest verdict
**EXPERIMENT:** coherent, accessible and grounded implementation that creates a new physical-paper response to the established mechanics. It is not a proven AAA art-quality result. A possible disconfirmation is that the extra paper contour competes with the historically authored central models or overwhelms a smaller screen. Test each chapter at full-size and narrow mobile viewport before asserting visual superiority.
