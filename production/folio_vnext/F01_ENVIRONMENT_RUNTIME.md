# F01 · Environmental staging

The public Living Book preserves the reviewed Blender GLB byte for byte. Its original Tiger, Wordstreamer, NiAlBu, three seat-mounted scored hinges, twelve articulated gusset links, hard-edged niche arches and separate idle tracks provide all the character work. The archived Atlas, official copy, testimony access rules, and non-WebGL understudy remain unchanged.

- **A** samples each station's complete set of authored Blender tracks in three nonoverlapping windows. Each actor counterfolds at its existing fixed foot as that station opens. Reverse follows the same sampled path in reverse, including a mid-gesture reversal; no geometry is moved between locations. With reduced motion, A shows the complete authored detent without the sweep.
- One shared-spine paper stage has three depth panels with exactly coincident adjacent edges, joined by two dark score bars. It opens just behind the printed control field, without replacing the source book or sitting in front of its labels.
- Each station has a local, differently cut shadow territory and a separately phased instance of its existing authored child idle. The dark tonal effects are physical geometry and negative space, with no colored lighting, sky motifs, glow, stars or particle effects.
- Two narrow physical links align the existing arches into one continuous profile only after NiAlBu has finished rising. B and C retain the independent authored detail and comparison clips; neither drives the environmental staging.
