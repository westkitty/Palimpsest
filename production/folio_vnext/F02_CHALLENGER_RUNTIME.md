# F02: Blender paper loom in the Living Book

The runtime mounts the paper loom from the reviewed `Palimpsest_F02_The_All_Unfolds.blend`, not a dynamic shader pretending to be its physical structure. The GLB contains the mirrored source hinges, the three real folded leaves, their actual applied black-through cut edges, six gussets from the evaluated source Hooks, four die-cut spiral strips and twenty bonded support feet, the original Blender engraved points, printed strips and raised Zentrum landmark. It uses the prepared laminae already depicted in the candidate. The arrangement is editorial, not proposed new canon.

The book and cut edges still arrive white. The original liquid ink spread, first points, clusters and dense galaxy masks still run on the vellum independently. Black stock in the exported loom is distinct from that original vellum substrate. Existing A/B/C and original progressive state, witness flight, source-authored Digital Geode and captive with inspection-only text gate are all preserved. The GLB has no inner inscription, source text or source-font binaries, table, shared book, controls, images, lights or cameras. The only visible title outline in the GLB is the existing public Zentrum name.

- Frames `1 / 39 / 65 / 110` represent no stars, first points, clusters and full galaxy. The actual Blender fold keys become a single 24 fps GLTF clip; its six source Hook meshes carry sampled morph positions. The transition staggers its stars without displaying future stages. Interaction only advances source-approved detents. Reduced motion settles all mechanisms to the selected detent.
- The original F02 surface remains useful even if the lazy-loaded same-origin GLB cannot load; the scenic paper fallback is kept in that case.
