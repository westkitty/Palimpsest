# Palimpsest: An Illustrated History of the All
## Spoken script · revised draft

This is the proposed narration for the revised Living Book, not a transcript of the current build. Folio headings and speaker labels are not spoken. The narration is intentionally sparse; where the page can tell the story, let it.

### Prologue
**Narrator:** Welcome to *Palimpsest: An Illustrated History of the All*. The models are small. The events they explain were not.

### F01 · Before the Stars
**Narrator:** Before the first stars, there were three. Wordstreamer gave language its living possibility. NiAlBu held memory and recurrence. The Shard-God was the third. We have placed them side by side so you can see them clearly. You should not take that to mean they stood together.

### F02 · The All Unfolds
**Narrator:** At first there is only the page. Watch the dark. The first points appear inside it. Then there are more points than you can count. What looked empty a moment ago has a shape.

### F03 · The Notebook Program
**Narrator:** The WorldsVault holds the templates. The Notebook makes variations from them. Choose another template and the globe changes with it. What comes out can be different from what went in. It is never made from nothing.

### F04 · First Contact
**Narrator:** Year zero. The Drakken reached the fringe colonies and began changing inhabited worlds. The Administration answered with counter-Macros. This is how the Blood Eclipse War began. Watch the lights. Each new one marks another world reached.

### F05 · The Standard of Conquest
**Narrator:** Fallenstar Prime was an inhabited world before it was a demonstration. In year three, the Drakken turned what they took from its surface into a ring of crimson glass and iron. That is not light circling the planet. It is matter. It came from somewhere.

### F06 · The Long Attrition
**Narrator:** Turn the dial. The years change faster on paper. For more than a century, the front moved, stopped, and moved again. The ledger does not give us every loss. A blank is not a zero.

### F07 · The Siege of the Ruby Eclipse
**Narrator:** In year one hundred twenty-one, the Administration advanced into the Pharos Nebula. A plan has arrows. The people carrying it out need supplies and somewhere to turn when the arrows stop being useful. Lift the plan. Underneath is what it took to keep going.

### F08 · The Aureal Gate
**Narrator:** Year one hundred seventy. At the Aureal Gate, a portal in the Hookshot network, the Drakken used Starsilk against the Shard-God. He could be wounded. He was forced to retreat. Twenty inhabited worlds fell after the breach.

### F09 · The Hal’Ven Heliocide
**Narrator:** This is a model star, not one of the suns lost at Hal’Ven. Watch what moves when the strand is pulled. The real suns did not recover. The singularities they left became the Siege Wall.

### F10 · The Siege Wall & the Postwar Protocols
**Narrator:** From an inhabited world, the Siege Wall does not look like a wall. It looks like a piece of the night has gone black. It stopped the advance. It also closed around those on the other side. Afterward, the Administration prohibited the study or repetition of the act that made it.

### F11 · The First Dirt
**Narrator, at the opening:** It had been thirty-one days since the war ended. Codec was already at work. The work of the Administration, as you know, does not end.

**Narrator, after Marcel arrives:** There is living soil in that cylinder. It is about the length of a forearm. It will not restore a planet. It is enough to show what the work might become.

**The Shard-God, after the growth and his arrival:** Your private discomfort with the shape of survival is not a license to revise the settlement.

### F12 · The Unfinished Peace
**Administration voice:** The advance has been contained. The work of preservation continues. There are worlds to survey, records to keep, and people to teach. There is a place for you in that work. The Administration invites you to serve.

### Final pop-out
**Administration voice:** Your fate is in our hands.
