## 1. The experience and its governing principle

**[ACCEPTED DIRECTION]** Palimpsest is an elaborate interactive, sculptural, three-dimensional pop-up book presented as an Administration-approved educational object intended to be read to children. It should inspire genuine wonder. Its official story and its concealed history coexist without the narrator ever revealing or reacting to the concealed material.

**[USER-DIRECTED — CRITICAL]** The narrator **must not acknowledge the dark truth**—not explicitly, not as an aside, not through an ominous change of tone, a knowing pause, a deliberate verbal clue, or a reaction when the player uncovers an inscription. The earlier assistant repeatedly crossed this line; those interpretations are superseded.

The approved narrative can discuss history, cosmic change, systems, and great achievements. The **player alone** makes any connection between that official narrative and hidden maker inscriptions. The same spoken content plays whether a player found a secret or not. **The revelation changes the reader's understanding, not the public-facing presentation.**

**[ACCEPTED DIRECTION]** Audio delivery takes its cue from *Your Sky Is Built from Your Dead*: a cheerful, reassuring, conversational mid-century public-information-film voice. It is **not** stern, solemn, theatrical, conspiratorial, campy, or a caricature of children's storytelling. The period orchestral approach may include light woodwinds, pizzicato strings, muted brass, and similar textures; individual folio cues remain to be authored. The original project's repository was not positively identified in the earlier GitHub lookup; do not overclaim that its precise voice performance has been auditioned or cloned.

**[CARRIED-FORWARD CONCEPT]** Mechanically, the reader turns large, physically plausible paper spreads, observes immense reversible pop-up structures rising from the page, and may inspect the sculptures by orbiting the camera. An optional miniature first-person mode lets them walk on the book's constructed spaces and discover tiny, concealed inscriptions. Miniature exploration and page-turning are separate modes. After a folio's introductory performance, the background music can continue while the reader explores. These are existing conversation-level design intentions, not a statement that the current build implements them all.

## 4. Folio three — Planetary templates, the World's Vault, and the Notebook Program

### User's correction of the initial pitch

**[USER-DIRECTED]** Most worlds were **procedurally generated**, rather than uniquely hand-created. Tiger **cannot create** in the relevant sense. He had access to planetary **templates held in the World's Vault**, and the majority of worlds were formed through their procedural recombination or variation. These are **not necessarily exact duplicate planets**; visible family resemblance and bounded variation are the important ideas.

**[USER-DIRECTED]** Folio three should explicitly establish **what is filling the All** and introduce the **Notebook Program** as the meaningful next mechanism. That matters because it sets up the subsequent emergence/introduction of the **Drakken**. Do not reduce this spread to a generic celebration of organically flourishing worlds.

### Revised experience pitch

**[PROPOSED — DIRECTION FAVORED, DETAILS UNAPPROVED]** Working title: **“The Wonderful World of the Notebook Program.”**

A vast, immaculate archival mechanism opens from the book. Planetary templates unfurl like elaborate celestial blueprints. The Notebook Program operates like an astonishingly precise printworks or mechanical compiler: source patterns generate successive planets, with recognizable topological similarities yet varied oceans, landforms, climates, and surface arrangements. The page becomes a demonstration of **cosmic procedure, reproduction, and controlled variation**, not divine spontaneous creation.

**Possible paper engineering ideas [PROPOSED]:**

- Nested blueprint leaves physically rise from the **World's Vault** and slot into a central planetary-forming mechanism.
- The same template can be reused with different folds, cutouts, orientations, or adjustments, making both repetition and difference visually intelligible.
- Three major generated worlds may remain as large inspectable pop-ups, but they should be presented as **outputs of a template system**, not unrelated free-form miracles.
- Once the demonstration settles, the Vault's template leaves retract, and the generated planets remain visible among the stars established on folio two.
- A later folio can build causally from this familiar Notebook Program process into the Drakken. Do not preemptively invent the precise next event or biological appearance here.

### Official narrator: possible voice, not approved script

**[PROPOSED SAMPLE]**

> “Now, making a great many worlds is quite a considerable undertaking! Fortunately, the Notebook Program has a splendid solution. With a carefully prepared collection of planetary templates, it can produce remarkable variety across the All. Rather clever, wouldn't you say?”

This conveys real procedural ingenuity with cheerful educational sincerity. It should not hint that Tiger is incapable of creation or that a secret has been concealed. Verify the wording against existing canon before recording; no final narration has been approved.

### Hidden inscription: alternatives considered

**[REJECTED / SUPERSEDED]** Original assistant proposal:

> “They were never empty.”

The user correctly objected that this refers to an unestablished or irrelevant premise. **Do not preserve it as the hidden truth of folio three.**

**[USER-DIRECTED IDEA]** The hidden observation should reveal that **Tiger took the planetary templates away and locked them up**, or equivalently expose his ownership/control of the means by which planets are produced. The user asked for a stronger alternative.

**[PROPOSED — NOT YET APPROVED]** Assistant's nine-word alternative:

> **He could not create. He locked away the templates.**

This combines two aspects: Tiger's limitation and his deliberate sequestration of the templates. It would appear **inside or near the miniature Vault**, through the same azure shimmer as other maker inscriptions. The narrator does **not** acknowledge the hidden allegation.

**[OPEN]** Decide whether the literal locking of the Vault should be visible as a routine **official animation** or only discoverable in miniature. The prior assistant offered that choice; the user has **not** answered it. Also determine whether “World's Vault” is the locked official name and whether the final hidden wording should focus on incapacity, theft, confinement, or restricted access. Do not silently elevate the proposed nine-word line into canon.

### Why this is narratively important

**[ACCEPTED ANALYSIS]** The significance of this spread lies in cause and consequence. The All is filled via a system of stored planetary templates and procedural production. The Notebook Program must be understood before the Drakken enter the narrative; they should arrive as a development in a system the reader has already witnessed, not as inexplicable monsters dropped into an unrelated history.

## 6. Creative and technical guardrails

1. **Protect the two distinct knowledge layers.** Official narration cannot discuss or knowingly insinuate the secret murder, NiAlBu's imprisonment, or hidden template sequestration. Even audience discovery must not trigger narrator reactions.
2. **Correct relationships every time.** NiAlBu = husband/he. Wordstreamer = wife/she. Wordstreamer is the murdered figure. The corrected second inscription uses **“her”** deliberately.
3. **No stars in folio one.** The first and grandest initial galactic reveal belongs to folio two. Any lingering older starfield in folio-one design is stale.
4. **Azure shimmer is the discovery signature.** Not golden; not a persistent game collectible; not a new public explanatory panel.
5. **Wonder stays sincere.** The galaxy and the generated planetary systems should be genuinely beautiful and worth inspecting independently of secret knowledge.
6. **Treat procedural generation as procedure, not unlimited creation.** The World's Vault supplies finite inherited templates. Repetition and bounded variation should be perceptible; exact procedural algorithm, available variants, and template origins require sourcing before being called canon.
7. **Notebook Program precedes Drakken explanation.** Use the sequence as conceptual scaffolding, not permission to invent the appearance or origins of individual Drakken strains.
8. **Distinguish an accepted premise from an approved line.** A good assistant suggestion does not become immutable canon merely because it was pitched.
9. **Preserve physical pop-up logic.** Dramatic celestial effects should visibly emerge through/around the book's paper mechanics; an interactive universe is not a conventional webpage covered in buttons.
10. **Read-only record.** This document neither certifies the current GitHub prototype nor authorizes edits, new official lore entries, model generations, or publication.
